# Evidence Participation

Participation is determined independently for one canonical Evidence artifact and one
valid Admission Evaluation Context.

Every canonical Evidence artifact participates in every valid Admission Evaluation
Context.

PARTICIPATES means only "available for later Admission consideration."

Participation does not imply relevance, authority, probative value, sufficiency,
favourability to retirement, or any verdict.

Participation does not modify Evidence or Admission Evaluation Context.

There is no NOT_ELIGIBLE outcome.

Invalid or incomplete Evidence and invalid or incomplete contexts are construction
failures, not participation outcomes.

Referent correspondence, retirement applicability, temporal applicability, relevance,
authority, sufficiency, conflicts, and verdict behavior remain deferred.
